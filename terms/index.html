2. How your booking works

No contract is formed on this website. All quotes are subject to availability and price changes until full payment or deposit is received and confirmed by us in writing. When you book through us, we act as an appointed retail agent for the relevant airline, tour operator or travel supplier. Your contract for the travel services is with that principal supplier, and their booking conditions apply alongside ours.

3. Financial protection (PTS and ATOL)

  • PTS trust protection. We are a member of Protected Trust Services (PTS Membership No. 6619). All client monies paid to us for non-flight arrangements, such as hotels, transfers or land-only tours, are held in an independent trust account managed by Protected Trust Services, in line with the Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018.
  • ATOL protection. Where you book an ATOL-protected flight-only ticket or flight-inclusive package through us via Aviate, we act as an agent for Aviate Management Ltd (ATOL 10232). You will be issued with an ATOL Certificate upon payment.

4. Service fees, changes and cancellations

Any applicable agency service or visa assistance fees will be disclosed to you before you commit to a booking and are non-refundable once work has commenced. Supplier cancellation and amendment fees apply in accordance with the principal supplier’s terms.

5. Passports, visas and travel insurance

You are responsible for ensuring all travellers hold valid passports, visas and health documentation. While we offer visa guidance and application checks, final visa decisions rest solely with the relevant embassy or consulate. We strongly recommend taking out comprehensive travel insurance at the time of booking.
