Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

Ginasworldboutique & Travel Ltd, trading as Gina’s Travel Agency (company number 10958121, registered office 81 Stone Lodge Road, Dartford, DA2 6GZ), is the data controller for personal information collected through ginastravel.co.uk. Contact us at info@ginastravel.co.uk.

2. What we collect

When you request a quote or book with us through our website, phone, email or WhatsApp, we collect your name, contact details, travel preferences and, when you book or ask for visa help, passport and passenger details.

If you complete our visa pre-assessment, we also collect the details you choose to give us about your family, work, finances, travel history and documents, so we can assess your options.

3. How we use your data

We use your information solely to prepare travel quotes, administer bookings, process visa assistance requests, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations such as PTS trust account reporting and ATOL certification.

4. Sharing your data

To fulfil your travel arrangements, we share the necessary passenger details with trusted airlines, tour operators (such as Aviate Management Ltd), hotels, visa centres and Protected Trust Services (PTS). We also use service providers to receive website enquiries, send emails and measure website visits without cookies. We never sell your data to third parties.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing info@ginastravel.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
