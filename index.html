Good to know

Questions we get every week.

Can I book directly on this website?
No. We are a consultant-led agency, so every booking is made by us on your behalf after you have approved the itinerary and price. Send an enquiry and we’ll come back with options.
Do you charge a fee for quotes?
Quotes are free. A small service fee applies to some bookings, such as complex multi-city itineraries and visa assistance, and we always tell you the fee before you commit.
Which visas can you help with?
UK visitor and family visas, Schengen, USA (ESTA and B1/B2), Canada, UAE, Türkiye, Nigeria and Ghana are the most common. We guide you through requirements and check your application; the decision is always made by the embassy. Send an enquiry and we will reply with a short pre-assessment to complete, so we can tell you what you qualify for and which documents to prepare.
Is my money protected?
Package holidays we arrange are booked through accredited, financially protected partners. For flight-only bookings we issue tickets through licensed consolidators and recommend travel insurance, which we can also arrange.
How quickly will I hear back?
We answer WhatsApp messages as quickly as we can during office hours (Mon–Sat, 9am–7pm UK time), and follow up with full itineraries and quotes shortly after.
